Description

ObjectiveExplore how different AI tools support prompting, research, custom assistants, and chatbot creation.
Tools & technologyClaude, ChatGPT, custom GPTs, Stormi AI, Chatbase, and presentation software.

Process

I tested Claude with repeated prompts, practiced with custom GPTs, used Stormi AI as a research assistant, and built a chatbot. When my first chatbot platform could not publish publicly, I adapted by moving the project to Chatbase.

Value Proposition of the Artifact

Unique value

The artifact shows adaptability across multiple AI platforms and my willingness to troubleshoot a project instead of stopping when the original approach failed.

Relevance

It is relevant to employers and educators because it demonstrates practical AI tool use, prompt development, research, chatbot creation, and problem-solving.
