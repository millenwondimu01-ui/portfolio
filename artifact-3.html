Artifact 3

Machine Learning vs. Deep Learning

This artifact compares traditional machine learning and deep learning through customer churn prediction and image recognition.

Read full artifact ↗

Description

ObjectiveExplain why different problems require different modeling approaches and connect each method to a real-world application.
Tools & technologyMachine learning concepts, CNN concepts, Spotify and Google Lens examples, Microsoft Word, and ChatGPT for research and text refinement.

Process

I compared the data requirements and strengths of traditional machine learning with deep learning. I used Spotify churn prediction to represent structured behavioral data and Google Lens image recognition to explain how convolutional neural networks work with complex image data.

Value Proposition of the Artifact

Unique value

The artifact connects model selection to practical business decisions instead of discussing the technologies only in theory.

Relevance

It demonstrates analytical thinking, an understanding of structured versus unstructured data, and the ability to explain why one technical approach is more appropriate than another.

References

AI-ML 500 course materials; Spotify and Google Lens as real-world examples; ChatGPT was used for research and text summarization.
