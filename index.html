Portfolio journey

From a class assignment to a portfolio I am proud to share.

Building this portfolio helped me see how each assignment reflects a different part of my growth in artificial intelligence, machine learning, leadership, and ethical decision-making. I began with Google Sites because it was familiar and easy to use, but I took time early in the course to learn GitHub because it is more widely used in the professional world. As the portfolio assignment came to an end, I decided it was the right time to make the switch.

I am happy with the final result because the GitHub portfolio feels cleaner, more professional, and easier to share. The process also pushed me to think carefully about how I explain my work, identify the skills behind each artifact, and present academic projects in a way that is relevant to future employers. I plan to continue updating the portfolio as I complete new projects and gain more experience in data analytics and AI.
